Calculation methods

Spans of the same sound that touch or overlap count once, including across segments. Different sounds may overlap. Rate = span count × 3600 ÷ analyzed seconds. Activity uses fixed 60-second bins, with ties resolved to the earliest bin. A cluster contains at least three spans, each within 10 seconds of the group’s end; it has no medical meaning. Window-center cells estimate timing. Short sessions give unstable hourly extrapolations.
